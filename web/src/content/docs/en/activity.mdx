## Open the calendar inside Discord

DisCalendar works as a Discord **Activity**. You can open your server's calendar inside Discord without switching to a browser.
This is handy when you gather in a voice channel to plan the next event.

1. In a server where the DisCalendar bot is installed, open a voice or text channel
2. Select **DisCalendar** from **Activities** (the rocket icon) in the message box or the voice channel
3. The first time, Discord asks you to sign in with your Discord account. Select **Authorize**

The calendar of the server you launched it from opens. To see another server's calendar, launch the Activity from that server.

## What you can do

Viewing, creating, editing, and deleting events work the same as in a browser. Restrictions on who can edit events ([Edit and delete events](/docs/edit)) still apply.

Links to pages outside the Activity, such as these docs or share links, open in your browser after Discord asks you to confirm.
Use DisCalendar in a browser for scheduling polls, All events, and switching servers.

<Callout type="info">
Screens are not synced between people in the same Activity. Each person works on their own screen, and saved changes appear after reloading.
</Callout>

## If it does not open

- If you see "Could not load the server", check that the bot is installed in the server and that you are a member of it
- The calendar cannot be opened in DMs or group DMs. Launch it from a server channel
- If you see "Could not sign in", reopen the Activity. If it keeps happening, open DisCalendar in your browser
- Some actions, such as uploading attachments, may not work because of Discord's restrictions. Use a browser for those
